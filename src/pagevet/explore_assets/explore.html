About this data


    

  • Field studies. For each topic, search results for ten or so shopping and software queries (saved results or a SearXNG instance) gave the URL list, at most four pages per site, not chosen by hand. Each page was fetched once over plain HTTP as a browser, with robots.txt respected, and compared with what GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot user agents were served. Pages that refused PageVet are counted as not read and were never retried another way.
    • 

  • Not read is not clean. A page PageVet could not read (blocked, robots.txt, error) is counted separately and never as "no findings".
    • 

  • Refusing AI crawlers is a site's choice. "Not served" records what a server answered a crawler's user agent; it is not a finding against the site. "Served different text" says only that the text differed; it does not say why.
    • 

  • Excerpts only. No page text is embedded beyond short excerpts of findings.
    • 

  • False-alarm pages are ordinary pages (homepages, articles, Wikipedia, news) scanned to check that PageVet does not flag them. Benchmark cases are PageVet's own evasion test: invented instructions, disguised; held-out sets were frozen before the patterns they test were written. BIPIA (Microsoft) is shown as its committed totals; its data is not redistributed.
    • 

  • Field studies record three AI crawlers. The fourteen-crawler check, robots.txt rules, llms.txt and noai signals come from pagevet-access (0.24.0) and show "not tested" for these studies.
